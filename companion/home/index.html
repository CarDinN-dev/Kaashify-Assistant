        Privacy

        More

        This asks Windows to leave Kaashify out of screen shares and recordings; the dashboard is never hidden. It is best effort, not a guarantee: some capture tools ignore the flag, a phone pointed at the screen sees everything, and the tray icon still shows in a full-desktop share. Using a hidden assistant in a proctored exam, an interview, or a recorded call can break rules and consent laws. It is built for notes, study, and accessibility. Turn it off to record a demo of the Companion.

        Drag Kaashify anywhere on the screen. It stays where you drop it, per display. In island mode it stays in the bar.
